# Dependency sources and notices

Exact direct dependencies are pinned in requirements.txt and requirements-build.txt.
Each upstream distribution includes its own licence; these are independent of
the retained MIT licence of the original thesis checker.

| PyMuPDF 1.28.2 / MuPDF | https://github.com/pymupdf/PyMuPDF/tree/1.28.2 and https://mupdf.com | AGPL-3.0 |

| ReportLab 4.5.1 | https://pypi.org/project/reportlab/4.5.1/#files | BSD |

upstream notices are retained by the packaging process. The Noto font licence
is bundled next to the font. Student documents are not part of corresponding source.
